# Does Gold Act as a True Safe Haven During S&P 500 Drawdowns?

**Course:** Advanced Mathematics (Vrije Universiteit Brussel)  
**Author:** Marcell Mayer

## Research Question
How strongly does gold (`GLD`) hedge against equity market downturns (`SPY`), and does the statistical relationship between the two assets shift significantly during extreme market stress?

## Data Source
Daily adjusted closing prices for `SPY` (S&P 500 ETF) and `GLD` (Gold ETF) from January 2016 to November 2026, downloaded via Yahoo Finance (`yfinance`).

## Files
- `fetch_data.py` – Python script that downloads the dataset.
- `market_data.csv` – The downloaded price dataset.# Advanced-Math
